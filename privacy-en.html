HANQIU · EXPIRY KEEPER

Privacy policy

Updated September 14, 2026. Expiry Keeper is provided by Hanqiu. We have no user accounts, ads, tracking, or servers collecting your item records.

Local storage

Item names, categories, dates, quantities, locations, notes, and photos stay in the app storage on your device. Version 1 does not offer iCloud sync and does not upload item records to the developer.

Photos and notifications

The app reads only images you select through the system photo picker and saves compressed copies. Reminders are local notifications requiring your permission. Notification text does not include item names. You can revoke notification permission in system settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payment for the one-time Premium purchase. The app uses StoreKit to verify your entitlement. The developer does not receive payment card details. See Apple’s privacy policy for transaction data handling.

Backups and deletion

System device backups may include app data; this is separate from in-app iCloud sync. Records remain until you delete them. Delete individual records or clear all data in Settings. Uninstalling removes local app data; manage system backups in device settings. You are responsible for exported CSV files.

Contact and hosting

If you email support, your address and message are used only to respond, not for marketing. Support emails are deleted when no longer needed; you can request deletion by email.
