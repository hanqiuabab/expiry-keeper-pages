HANQIU · EXPIRY KEEPER

Help and support

Expiry Keeper helps track expiry dates for food, medicine, cosmetics, and household items. Dates depend on the packaging information you enter; records do not replace packaging instructions, storage requirements, or professional advice.

Adding items

Tap plus on the Items tab and enter a name, category, and expiry date. You can calculate expiry using production date and shelf life. When an opened shelf life is supplied, the earlier expiry date is used.

How reminders work

Free reminders occur 3 days before expiry and on the day at 09:00. Premium supports up to 5 reminder points. Reminders at the same time are combined. Enable system notifications and open the app regularly to replenish the schedule. Focus modes, permission, and system settings can affect delivery. Expired items remain visible in the app.

Restoring Premium

Sign in with the Apple Account used for your purchase and tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen. Premium is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal. Email support with any error details; do not send payment card information.

Managing your data

Archive used-up or discarded items. Archived items do not count toward the 30-item free limit and receive no reminders. Restoring an item checks the limit again. Premium can export CSV. Version 1 has no import or cross-device sync; take care before uninstalling.
